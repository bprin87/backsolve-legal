Backsolve Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 September 2026

Your puzzles stay on your device

Backsolve does not require an account. Puzzle generation, word lists, answer checking, and practice mode work on your device without a gameplay server.

The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or a mechanism for sending puzzle answers or game history to the developer.

Local settings

Backsolve stores your annotation display preference, optional theme preference, haptic preference, selected practice difficulty, and whether you have seen the introductory guide on your device. The most recent completed daily result is saved locally in UserDefaults, including its entry date, submitted word, give-up status and difficulty. Practice results last only for the current app session. The current streak, longest streak, last completed UTC daily key and cumulative completed-day count used for difficulty progression are also saved locally. Both wins and losses count; practice does not. No cloud sync is implemented. Your streak is saved on this device only. Reinstalling the app, or setting up a new iPhone without restoring from backup, will reset it to zero. Only the latest completed daily result is retained; there is no full puzzle history.

Paid download

Backsolve is purchased once through the App Store. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions. The app does not request or receive your payment-card details. Apple handles the download purchase under its own terms.

Sharing

When you choose Share or Copy, Backsolve prepares a text summary or illustrated note containing the puzzle identifier, outcome and, for daily results only, current streak. It does not include the secret word or the letters in the clue rows. The app or service you choose to share through handles that content under its own terms.

Support

If you contact the developer for help, the information you choose to send is handled for the purpose of responding to your request.

Support contact: letsgetcreativedeveloper@proton.me

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. Any future update will state its effective date above.
